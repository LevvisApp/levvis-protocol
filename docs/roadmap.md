# Roadmap

Phases are ordered by dependency, not by date. No dates are promised until they are stated.

## Phase 0: Foundations (shipped in this repo)

- Public site with live Robinhood Chain readout (block height, gas, TVL, pairs)
- Token marquee fed by DexScreener profiles, sanitised before display
- Register of external facts with explicit states
- Fixture-driven gate suite covering layout at five breakpoints, links, motion and sanitising
- Brand palette and icon sampled from supplied assets

## Phase 1: Product surface

- Handle intake flow
- Four-card result view: Content, Behaviour, Presence, Audience
- Save and share formats for cards

## Phase 2: Comparison

- Side-by-side view of two accounts
- Stable card layouts so differences are readable at a glance

## Phase 3: On-chain layer

- Contract address announced and recorded in the register
- Access and settlement rules documented in `docs/tokenomics.md`
- Explorer links for every contract

## Phase 4: Ecosystem

- Public documentation for integrators
- Community-proposed card types through the contribution process

Items move between phases as dependencies change. Updates are made here in the same change that alters the plan.
